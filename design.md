# GI STORE — Design System

## Brand
- **Name:** GI STORE
- **Tagline:** Кімоно для перемог

## Colors
- Background: #0F0F0F (near-black)
- Surface: #1A1A1A (dark cards)
- Surface-2: #242424 (slightly lighter cards)
- Red accent: #E8232A (primary CTA, highlights)
- Red hover: #C41E24
- White: #FFFFFF
- Gray text: #A0A0A0
- Border: #2E2E2E

## Typography
- Headings: "Unbounded", sans-serif (Google Fonts) — bold, uppercase feel
- Body: "Inter", sans-serif
- Hero H1: 56px–72px, font-weight 900
- Section H2: 36px–48px, font-weight 800
- Card titles: 18px–22px, font-weight 700
- Body: 15px–16px, line-height 1.6

## Spacing
- Section padding: py-20 (80px top/bottom)
- Container max-width: 1280px, px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8
- Card gap: gap-6

## Buttons
- Primary: bg-red (#E8232A), text-white, px-8 py-4, rounded-sm, font-bold, uppercase tracking-wider
- Secondary: border border-white text-white, px-8 py-4, rounded-sm, hover:bg-white/10
- Ghost: text-red, no border, hover:underline

## Cards
- Background: #1A1A1A
- Border: 1px solid #2E2E2E
- Rounded: rounded-lg
- Hover: scale-[1.02], border-color: #E8232A transition

## UI Patterns
- Red left-border accents on section headings
- Full-bleed dark hero sections
- White product sections (for contrast/readability)
- Sticky top header with blur backdrop
- Floating bottom CTA bar on mobile (Telegram + Viber)
- Section labels: small uppercase red text above H2

## Icons
- Lucide React

## Images
- Product photos: white background or dark studio
- Hero: full-bleed dark dramatic photo of martial artist
